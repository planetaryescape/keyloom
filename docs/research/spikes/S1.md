# S1: which Moonlander revision is BK's (unresolved)

Question: is BK's Moonlander rev A (USB product ID `0x1969`) or rev B (`0x1972`)? The blueprint's S1 row says rev A. The S42 result in [15-open-questions.md](../../blueprint/15-open-questions.md) says rev B. Nothing in the evidence settles it.

## What was run

On 2026-10-15, while BK's Moonlander was connected, the build ledger records that the agent read its USB identity: vendor `0x3297`, product `0x1969`, "Moonlander Mark I". Recorded in the build ledger, not re-checked: the raw `system_profiler` output was not kept, so that reading cannot be inspected.

## What the evidence shows

- ZSA's QMK fork lists `zsa/moonlander/reva` with `pid 0x1969` and `zsa/moonlander/revb` with `pid 0x1972` (`keyboard.json` in the pinned fork checkout, `93b2b9ec3368` per [S5](S5.md)).
- keyloom built BK's layout for `reva` (build `bld_01M4ZXEY4HY9E2DAK6KN9QYHCR`, `board_revision: reva`) and flashed it through Zapp on 2026-10-15, 14:04 to 14:19Z. Zapp printed `Firmware loaded: STM32 DFU (67336 bytes)`, the board took it, and keyloom verified it running that build over raw HID. A rev A DFU image names the stock STM32 bootloader (`0483:df11`, [S11](S11.md)).
- That is consistent with rev A. It is not proof: whether Zapp refuses an image whose DFU suffix names a different bootloader is still open in S11.
- The S42 result states that BK's running Moonlander is `revb`, `0x3297:0x1972`. That section also says the Moonlander was not plugged in during the S42 run, and no log or output in the evidence shows a `0x1972` reading from his board. It reads as a deduction from keyloom's `revb` definition (`boards/zsa/moonlander/board.json`, `detect.usbProductId`), not an observation.

## What is still open

The answer. It needs `system_profiler SPUSBDataType -json` (or `keyloom doctor` / `keyloom keyboard detect`) run with BK's Moonlander plugged in, and the product ID and product string kept. Until then, both the rev A answer in S1 and the rev B statement in S42 are unverified, and the decision must not be taken from either.
