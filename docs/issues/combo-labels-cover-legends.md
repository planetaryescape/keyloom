# Combo labels cover key legends when combos are shown

With "Show combos" on, combo output labels sit on the line's midpoint, which for neighbouring keys is on top of the caps' own legends ("Spac" under the Esc-combo label, the ⇧ caps on the Moonlander's home row; evidence `rung15/thumb-combos-light-1440.png`). Place labels in the gap between caps or offset them outward, and fade the caps' legends under a label rather than covering them.
